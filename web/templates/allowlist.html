Allow specific domains to reach the internet. Matching is suffix-based: adding tesla.com (or *.tesla.com) permits tesla.com and all subdomains, but adding mail.google.com does NOT permit drive.google.com. Leading *. is optional and automatically stripped. When a client resolves an allowed domain, its IP is auto-added to the nftables allow set.
